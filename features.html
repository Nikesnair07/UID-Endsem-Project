Can I use Greek yogurt instead of sour cream?
Absolutely! Greek yogurt is a great 1:1 substitute for sour cream. It adds the same tangy flavour with more protein. For baking, use full-fat Greek yogurt for best results. 👍
What about in a cheesecake?
Yes! Use full-fat Greek yogurt. The texture will be slightly lighter but still delicious and creamy. 🎂
